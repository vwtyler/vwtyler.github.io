Hi, I'm Tyler.

I document real-world engineering work: community radio infrastructure at KAAD-LP, Linux/Liquidsoap streaming, and homelab systems that need to be reliable in practice, not just in theory.

This site is where I share configs, command-level walkthroughs, failures, fixes, and lessons learned so others (and future me) can reuse them.

Some of the projects I've written about:

- [Radiant](https://github.com/vwtyler/radiant), a self-hosted community radio operations platform, and [its deployment architecture](/posts/radiant-separating-code-and-deployment/).
- [Liquidsoap broadcasting](/posts/winamp-shoutcast-dsp-to-liquidsoap/) and [Icecast metadata automation](/posts/icecast-metadata-systemd-service/) at KAAD-LP.
- [Cloudflare dynamic DNS on Linux](/posts/cloudflare-ddns-systemd-timer/) for my homelab.
- [Developing Radiant with ChatGPT and GitHub](/posts/building-radiant-with-chatgpt-and-github/).
